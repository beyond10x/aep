## Consumer gap
The brain consumer has explicit operator authority to continue repair without spending ceilings. Its installed AEP variant requires numeric run and reservation terms and forwards the reservation as a native Claude session cap. The public driver also requires numeric total-run authority. Neither boundary currently expresses absent total and native session spending ceilings while retaining a separate conservative reservation estimate.

## Requested contract
Provide an explicit opt-in for uncapped total and native-session spending. Keep pre-launch accounting durable, retain exact actual charges and unknown reservations, and preserve run identities, failure outcomes, original resume terms, selected authentication, model controls, plugin hooks and independent evidence gates. Default missing authority must still refuse. Conflicting capped/unlimited flags and ambiguous resumes must remain explicit refusals. Do not clear ledgers, treat unknown as zero, bypass the driver or disguise a huge finite amount as infinity.

Synthetic tests must cover ordinary capped compatibility, explicit unlimited launch, reservation-versus-authority separation, response cost above the estimate, unknown cost across restart, contradictory arguments and absence of a native cap only under explicit authority. The owning repository agents choose the contract and perform implementation, review and release. The brain consumer validates a published compatible release later; this request authorizes no implementation or release by the brain session.
